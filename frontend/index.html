Heart Disease Risk Prediction

Enter 23 health details and a machine-learning model estimates the probability of heart disease. It is a student project built to learn how classification models work on healthcare data.

Educational project. The result is a model estimate, not a medical diagnosis.

How it works

Three steps, from your numbers to a probability.

  1. Enter health details

    Answer 23 questions in four short steps: age, blood pressure, blood test results, and exercise and lifestyle. Each one is explained on the Parameters page.

  2. Machine learning model

    A LightGBM classifier, trained on records of people with and without heart disease, reads your values.

  3. Risk prediction

    You get a prediction and two probabilities, disease and no disease, exactly as the API returns them.

Chosen from four models

I trained Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost and LightGBM on the same data split and compared them. LightGBM had the highest accuracy: 90.17% on 1,800 test records that were kept out of training.

  • Final modelLightGBM classifier with 300 trees, learning rate 0.05 and maximum depth 8.
  • Data9,000 records: 7,200 for training and 1,800 for testing.
  • Also measuredConfusion matrices for every model, and ROC-AUC where it was calculated.
