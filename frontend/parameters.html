Parameters that matter most to the model

Feature importance shows how much a feature contributed to the model’s decision-making process. It does not mean that this feature alone causes heart disease.

The score is the number of times LightGBM used a feature to split the data, counted across all of its trees. A feature can score high simply because it takes many different values.
